smartpen-bridge: UX review for a read-first app

v2.10.0 · reviewed 2026-10-19 · nothing in the repo was changed. Mockups are illustrative HTML with synthetic ink (not your handwriting).

The app's frame was built for capture: a header of pen and save controls, and a left panel of stroke lists. Book View, the Timeline and the Dates grid now do most of the daily work, and they sit inside that frame. The reading surface is the newer and better-looking part of the app, but it only gets what space the capture frame leaves over. The recommendations below move the app's weight toward reading, and leave capture and edit one deliberate step away.

What I looked at

I ran the real app (a scratch copy of your data, in an isolated profile, so your running app and real files were untouched) and captured it at 1600×950 and 1280×760: Timeline, Books home, a two-page spread, Transcript mode, all five left tabs, Transcript search, and the empty Editor. I also read App.svelte, Header, LeftPanel, TimelineFeed, DatesTab and ARCHITECTURE.md.

Not covered: anything needing a live pen, MyScript calls, or a populated Editor canvas (the Review flow, point editing, transcription runs). Search for "gift" returned "no matches in 116 transcribed pages". I did not check whether that is correct, so I make no search-quality claims here.

What is already working (keep it)

The two-page spread is calm and legible. Hover-to-reveal capture time (Times), the lazy Timeline feed, and the Dates heat grid are real strengths. "Load into Editor" as the deliberate crossing from reading to editing is the right model. Everything below builds on these.

Findings, ranked by how much they cost a reader

#FindingEvidence
1The reading surface is the smallest thing on screen.At 1280×760 the header (~170 px) + view bar + feed header leave about 500 px of reading height. At 1600 the left panel takes 450 px and the feed column is ~760 px wide. The page is capped at 1600 px, so wider monitors gain only margin.
2The header is a capture toolbar that is mostly dead while reading.Connect · Fetch Notes · Save (Strokes) · Delete (0) · Undo · Clear · Transcribe (0/0) are always shown; most are disabled in every Book View screenshot. At 1280 they collapse to unlabelled icons.
3The left panel is unrelated to what you are reading.Over a spread it shows "Connect pen and write to see strokes". The Transcripts tab shows "Select strokes and click Transcribe…". Its tabs describe capture; reading controls are only in the Dates tab.
4Three tiers of mode switches plus five tabs.Editor│Book View → Books│Timeline → Paper, Home, Strokes│Transcript, Spread, Times, Newest first, plus Strokes│Transcripts│Pages│Books│Dates on the left. That is 11+ choices before reaching a page, and "Books" means two different things (right pane vs. left tab).
5The same information and actions repeat.Timeline: "Load … into Editor" appears four times (left, feed header, day, card). Range appears in the left inputs and as "Change range". The book filter is in both places. "947 strokes · 2 pages" appears three times. "1 days" is a plural bug.
6Pages tab and Books home are the same list in two designs.Both enumerate the same 7 books and page counts; neither shows when a book was written or how much is transcribed.

1Slim the header; let capture tools appear when relevant high impactsmall effort

Problem. Finding 1, 2. A 110 px header (170 px when it wraps) with seven mostly-disabled buttons, on a screen you now mostly read.

Recommendation. One 44 px row: product name (also drop the stale "Stroke Bridge"/arrow wording), the primary navigation (rec. 2), search, a single pen chip (state + battery + a Connect click target), and Settings. The capture group (Save, Undo, Delete, Clear, Transcribe) lives in a capture bar that shows when the Editor is active, when a pen is connected, or when anything is unsaved, with the unsaved amber dot always visible on the chip. Folder status moves into Settings with a warning dot on the gear only when it is not ready.

2One navigation spine, and view options in one place high impactmedium effort

Problem. Finding 4. The user's decision tree has three levels of mutually exclusive modes plus loose toggles scattered across two bars.

Recommendation. Make the three things you actually do the top-level destinations, and name them by task, not by internals:

  • Library: Books home → book → pages/spread (today's Book View → Books).
  • Timeline: the date feed (today's Book View → Timeline, with Dates folded in; rec. 4).
  • Editor: capture and edit (rename the pane "Editor"; keep tab ids unchanged as the code notes require).

Per-view toggles (Paper, Times, Spread/Single, Newest first, Strokes│Transcript) move out of the top bar into a single View ▾ popover on the pane's own toolbar, so each view shows only the options that apply to it. The current mode-switch buttons then stop needing the "unsaved edits" confirm in three separate places; one guarded route change does it.

4Timeline: one range control, one load action high impactmedium effort

Problem. Finding 5. Setting a range takes two places (grid/inputs on the left, "Change range" in the feed). "Load into Editor" is offered at range, day, card and left-panel level at once, and each repeats counts the reader has already seen.

Recommendation.

  • Put a horizontal activity strip across the top of the feed (the heat grid, turned sideways, with the visible range as a draggable window). It doubles as scroll-spy. The date inputs and Latest/1/7/30 presets become one Range ▾ menu next to it. The left panel can then be collapsed in Timeline like everywhere else.
  • Sticky day headers with the date, counts and a single Edit day… overflow. Cards keep Open page and Whole page, and put "Load into Editor" under a ⋯ menu. The range-level load moves to that same top bar as a quiet secondary button.
  • Fix "1 days" (pluralise), and show the summary once, in the top bar.
  • Day density at a glance: the card header shows 9:05–9:42 · 37 min already; add the page chips ("Study Notes 3 · P2, P3") as links to the reader, and a one-line transcript excerpt for a card's page when that page is transcribed.
The transcript excerpt needs care: stored lineIds are unreliable for pre-Aug-2026 pages and a card is a sitting, not a page. Use page-level text only, label it "Page transcript", and keep it collapsed by default.

5Library home: notebooks as covers, not a collapsed list medium impactmedium effort

Problem. Finding 6, 7. The app's best visual asset (page thumbnails) is hidden behind a click, and the home view contains no sense of when or how much you wrote. Yet the capture-date index already knows both.

Recommendation. Home is a shelf of notebook cards: alias, volume badge, page count, first–last written dates, last written, a thin transcribed % bar and a three-page thumbnail fan (the most recently written pages, not P1). Sort chips: Recently written · Name · Most pages. Opening a book shows today's thumbnail grid, with a date badge on each card and a small ✓ for transcribed, plus a "Jump to page…" field. "Recent" stays, but sits above the shelf when non-empty.

6The page reader: one toolbar, a position, and ink beside text high impactmedium effort

Problem. Finding 8. While reading you cannot tell where you are in the book, you cannot see text next to its ink, and each page repeats a full toolbar. The capture-date information the app now tracks isn't shown on the page you are reading, except through hover.

Recommendation.

  • One shared toolbar above the spread (zoom/Fit apply to both pages by default, rotate and orientation per page via a small ⟲ on the page corner). Per-page header keeps just title, stroke count and written date.
  • Position and jump: "P101–P103 of 111", a page slider or Go to page…, ←/→ keys (and J/K for next/previous transcribed page if cheap).
  • Written-on ribbon under each page title: "Written Mar 10, 2025" or day chips when the page spans several; hovering a chip highlights that day's ink (what Times does now), clicking opens that day in Timeline. Today this is discoverable only by hovering ink with Times on.
  • Ink + text: add a third state beside Ink and Text. On a single page, ink is left and text right; in a spread, text sits under the ink. Where a line's yBounds are trustworthy (post-Aug 2026), hovering a line highlights its ink band, a natural reuse of the existing preview matching. For older pages, show side-by-side with no linking.
  • Transcript pane empty state: replace "No transcript for this page yet. [Add transcript]" with Transcribe this page only when MyScript is configured, since "Add transcript" reads as typing it by hand.

7Search that you can reach from anywhere medium impactmedium effort

Problem. Finding 9. Reading is about finding things, and the one full-text search sits in a left tab named for a capture workflow, two modes deep. A reader won't look for it there.

Recommendation. A search field (and Ctrl K) in the top bar, using the same transcript-search.js index plus a lightweight parse of dates and notebook names ("Mar 2025", "Study Notes 1"). Results are grouped (Pages · Notebooks · Days), each with a snippet, a thumbnail and the written date; Enter opens the page in the reader with the matched line highlighted (when line→ink is trustworthy), and "Load into Editor" stays a secondary action. The existing left-panel search can remain inside the Editor's context.

8Colour roles and small polish medium impactsmall effort

Give each state its own colour

Finding 10. Right now the same pink-red means "primary", "selected" and (darkened) "destructive". For a viewer where you are constantly toggling things on and off, selection should not shout: use a neutral raised style with a blue underline for on, keep the red accent for the single primary action on a screen, and keep red-dark only for deletion.
